What Users Are Reporting About ProffeInvest

ProffeInvest offers investment newsletters and stock recommendations to German-speaking investors. User complaints focus on performance claims that do not match actual results, with subscribers reporting that recommended investments have performed significantly worse than advertised.

Subscription and billing issues are also reported, with some users describing difficulty cancelling subscriptions and being charged for additional services they did not request. Refund requests are reportedly denied based on terms and conditions that users claim were not clearly presented.

Before subscribing to ProffeInvest, verify whether the service holds appropriate investment advisory licenses in Germany. Investment newsletters are not always regulated as financial advice, and subscribers should understand the limitations of the service.

User complaints about ProffeInvest have been documented on Trustpilot, where multiple victims have shared similar experiences. We recommend reading these reviews as part of your due diligence before engaging with any unregulated platform.

Some fraudulent platforms copy the name, registration number, or branding of legitimate regulated firms. Always verify the exact URL, phone number, and contact details against the official regulator's website, not information provided by the firm itself.
