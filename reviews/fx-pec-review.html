What Users Are Reporting About FX PEC

FX PEC presents itself as a forex and commodities broker. User complaints describe a platform with manipulated pricing, where spreads widen dramatically during trades and stop-loss orders are triggered by price spikes that do not appear on independent market data.

Withdrawal issues are a major theme, with users reporting that withdrawal requests are either ignored or met with demands for additional documentation, fees, and verification steps that were not mentioned during the deposit process.

FX PEC cannot be verified with any major financial regulator. The company claims made on the website do not match any entries in the FCA, BaFin, ASIC, or CySEC registers. Users are advised to avoid depositing funds with this platform.

User complaints about FX PEC have been documented on Trustpilot, where multiple victims have shared similar experiences. We recommend reading these reviews as part of your due diligence before engaging with any unregulated platform.

How to Verify FX PEC's Regulatory Status

Before trusting any broker or trading platform with your funds, verify their regulatory status using these official databases:

  • FCA Register (UK): search the exact company name
  • SEC EDGAR (US): check for registration
  • FINRA BrokerCheck (US)
  • ASIC Connect (Australia)
  • CySEC (Cyprus/EU)
  • CFTC registration check

⚠️ Clone Firm Warning

Some fraudulent platforms copy the name, registration number, or branding of legitimate regulated firms. Always verify the exact URL, phone number, and contact details against the official regulator's website, not information provided by the firm itself.
