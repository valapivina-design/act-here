Financial & Digital-Asset Disputes

Civil strategy for crypto and fintech disputes, tracing support, jurisdiction analysis, and proportionate litigation.

Typical instructions

Clients instruct us after exchange freezes, failed withdrawals, rug-pull style collapses, or intermediary negligence. We focus on recoverable defendants, forum, and interim tools, not empty promises of full recovery.

What we examine

  • Contractual and tort pathways against identifiable parties
  • On-chain evidence quality and expert support
  • Freezing injunctions and disclosure applications where justified
  • Settlement leverage versus litigation cost
