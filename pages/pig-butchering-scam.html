Long-Game Investment Disputes

High-value investment schemes that cultivate trust over months create complex multi-jurisdictional disputes. We focus on evidence, forum, and civil leverage.

Why these cases need litigation discipline

Facts often span messaging apps, offshore entities, and digital wallets. Early preservation and a sober defendant analysis matter more than slogans about “recovery.”

Our approach

  • Timeline and document reconstruction
  • Identification of solvent or reachable defendants
  • Regulatory notifications where useful to the civil case
  • Negotiation or proceedings with clear fee gates
