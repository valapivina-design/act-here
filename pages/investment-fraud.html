Commercial & Investment Litigation

Counsel for contract, shareholder, and investment disputes, from early case assessment through settlement or trial.

Matters we handle

Contract & commercial disputes

Supply failures, payment disputes, exclusivity breaches, and termination fights. We map leverage early so negotiation is grounded, not hopeful.

Investment & financial product disputes

Losses tied to unsuitable advice, misleading promotions, unregulated platforms, or broken exit terms. We assess civil claims alongside regulatory notification options.

Shareholder & board conflicts

Unfair prejudice petitions, deadlock, and director duties, handled with commercial realism about cost and control.

How instruction works

  • Pre-action protocol correspondence where required
  • Mediation or without-prejudice negotiation
  • County Court or High Court proceedings when needed
