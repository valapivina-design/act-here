About Act Here

We exist to bring clarity to complex disputes, so clients can decide, instruct, and resolve with confidence.

Why Act Here

Litigation should not feel opaque. Too often, clients receive dense advice, shifting fee estimates, and no clear sense of the next decision. Act Here was built to reverse that pattern.

We are a United Kingdom litigation practice focused on commercial, consumer, and complex dispute matters. Our counsel is plain-spoken, our engagement letters are specific, and our advocacy is disciplined from first instruction through resolution.

What We Do

Commercial Litigation

Contract breaches, shareholder conflicts, supplier disputes, and board-level matters handled with strategy that boards can actually follow.

Consumer & Financial Claims

Mis-selling, unfair contract terms, investment disputes, and consumer redress pathways pursued with proportionate, evidence-led advocacy.

Dispute Resolution

Negotiation, mediation, arbitration, and High Court or County Court proceedings, structured to resolve efficiently while preserving leverage.

Transparent Fees

Fixed-fee options where the scope is clear; hourly or hybrid models where complexity demands flexibility, always set out before work begins.

How We Work

  • Clear engagement letters. Scope, timeline, and fee structure in plain English before any chargeable work starts.
  • Honest assessments. We will tell you if a claim is weak, disproportionate, or better resolved commercially.
  • Evidence first. Strategy follows documents, timelines, and jurisdiction, not optimism.
  • Client control. You approve major steps. We do not surprise you with undisclosed workstreams.
  • Confidentiality. Matter details stay within the retainer and applicable professional rules.
