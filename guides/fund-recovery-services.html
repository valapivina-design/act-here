Fund Recovery Services: How to Get Money Back From a Scam Broker

You lost money to a scam broker. Then a 'fund recovery specialist' contacts you — they found you through the same scam's victim list — and promises to get your money back for an upfront fee. This is the second scam, and it is often run by the same people as the first.

The recovery scam

How fake fund recovery services work

  • ✓They contact you — you never contacted them. Real recovery firms don't cold-call victims.
  • ✓They guarantee recovery, or quote a success rate above 90%. No honest investigator guarantees anything.
  • ✓They demand an upfront fee before any work begins, often framed as a 'refundable deposit' or 'court fee'.
  • ✓They claim to have 'inside contacts' at banks or regulators, or to have 'already located' your funds.
  • ✓They pressure you to act immediately, before you have time to verify them.
What real recovery looks like

The legitimate routes that actually work

Card chargeback and bank recall

The fastest legitimate route for card-funded deposits. Your bank reverses the transaction under 'services not as described' or fraud rules. The window is typically 120 days from the charge — file it immediately.

Crypto tracing

A forensic blockchain trace follows your payment from your wallet through the operator's addresses to the exchange where they cash out. Exchanges freeze accounts and cooperate when presented with a court-ready evidence file. This is the strongest route for crypto payments.

Civil litigation

A UK civil claim for fraud or breach of contract is available for up to 6 years. For amounts over £10,000, litigation counsel is the realistic route. For multi-victim platforms, coordinated claims change the economics and the regulator's attention.

How to verify a recovery service

The three checks before you pay anyone

  • ✓Did they contact you, or did you contact them? Real firms don't cold-call.
  • ✓Do they guarantee recovery? No honest investigator does — the answer should be 'we assess, then we tell you what's realistic'.
  • ✓Can you verify their company registration, their litigation counsel's SRA number, and their physical office? Check Companies House and the SRA register directly.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Are fund recovery services legitimate?

Some are — but the industry is saturated with scams. The test: did they contact you (red flag), do they guarantee recovery (red flag), and can you verify their company registration and litigation counsel independently (essential). How much does fund recovery cost?

Legitimate services work on a consultation basis, then a fee structure tied to the actual work: a fixed fee for a case assessment, then either hourly or a success-based percentage for the recovery work itself. Any demand for a large upfront 'refundable deposit' before any work is done is a red flag.

Can I get my money back from a scam broker?

Sometimes. The realistic routes are card chargeback (within 120 days), bank recall (within days), crypto tracing (permanent trail), and civil litigation (6-year limitation). The odds drop every week — which is why the first move is to preserve evidence and get a professional assessment while every route is still open.
