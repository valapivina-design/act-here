Investment Dispute Patterns in 2024

Investment schemes that cultivate trust over long periods generate distinctive litigation problems: fragmented evidence, offshore counterparties, and claimants who paid in stages under escalating pressure.

What to document early

  • Full chat and email exports
  • Every payment reference and wallet address
  • Marketing claims relied upon
  • Identity documents requested by the counterparty

Litigation reality

Not every loss yields a solvent defendant. Early case assessment exists to separate viable civil pathways from expensive dead ends, before fees accumulate.
